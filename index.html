三、主要研究方向

  • 生成式人工智能与城市空间智能优化：生成式AI、可控生成、城市街景优化、城市更新与环境评价。
  • 增强现实与数字城市设计：AR、WebAR、VR等技术在城市设计、空间决策及公众参与中的应用。
  • 城市大数据与主动出行：基于GPS轨迹、空间大数据及环境数据开展步行、骑行行为与城市空间研究。
  • 城市步行性与宜居环境评价：研究建成环境、城市空间品质与居民行为之间的关系。
  • 人居环境可持续设计：建筑、景观及城市公共空间的可持续设计策略与评价方法。

3. Research Areas

  • Generative AI and Intelligent Optimization of Urban Space: generative AI, controllable generation, streetscape improvement, urban regeneration, and environmental assessment.
  • Augmented Reality and Digital Urban Design: applications of AR, WebAR, and VR in urban design, spatial decision-making, and public participation.
  • Urban Big Data and Active Mobility: walking, cycling, and urban space research using GPS trajectories, large-scale spatial data, and environmental data.
  • Urban Walkability and Livable Environment Assessment: relationships between the built environment, urban spatial quality, and residents' behavior.
  • Sustainable Design of Human Settlements: sustainable design strategies and assessment methods for buildings, landscapes, and urban public spaces.
